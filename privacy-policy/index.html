Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how BookWorkGo ("we," "us") handles information when you visit bookworkgo.com, request a service, apply as a service pro, or opt in to our text messages. It works together with our Terms of Service.

While the website is in preview: details you type into the website's forms stay in your browser. They are not sent to us, and they disappear when you leave or reload the page unless you choose to download a draft file to your own device.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, company or property name, email address, and phone number.
  • Job details: property city and ZIP code, unit size, condition, preferred dates, notes, and communications about a job, including completion photos.
  • Service pro details: business name, service area, crew capacity, insurance status, experience, rates, and availability.
  • Text message consent: whether you opted in, when, and how, plus your opt-out requests.

Please don't send us door codes, lockbox codes, payment card numbers, or sensitive documents through website forms.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like most websites, those providers receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site. BookWorkGo does not add its own analytics or advertising trackers to this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to requests, agree on scope, price, and appointments, and coordinate jobs.
  • To review service pro applications and offer jobs.
  • To send the text messages you've opted in to, and to honor opt-out requests.
  • To follow up on completed work and handle questions or concerns.
  • To keep records, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

We may use software, including artificial intelligence tools, to help organize requests and schedules. People at BookWorkGo confirm job details with you.

How we share it

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • Between customers and service pros: the details a service pro needs to do a job (for example, the property's city, unit details, appointment time, and access arrangements), and the details a customer needs about the assigned service pro.
  • Vendors who help us operate: for example, providers of hosting, email, and text messaging, who may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BookWorkGo, our users, or others.
  • Business changes: as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Text messages

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the vendors that deliver our text messages on our behalf.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. See the text updates page and the text message terms.

Cookies and tracking

BookWorkGo doesn't set cookies or use browser storage on this website, and doesn't use analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking tools here. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep information as long as needed to coordinate your requests or jobs, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Records of text message consent and opt-outs are kept as long as needed to show we honored them.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold, and we limit access to people who need it to coordinate jobs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing BookWorkGoGo@gmail.com. We may need to keep some records where the law requires.
  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Stop other messages by telling us you'd like to opt out.

Children

BookWorkGo serves businesses and adults. Our website and services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we'll let affected people know.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: email BookWorkGoGo@gmail.com, or reply HELP to any BookWorkGo text message.
