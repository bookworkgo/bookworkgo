01 — The service

Vacant unit cleaning, arranged for you.

Our first service is ordinary cleaning of vacant rental units — between tenants, before a showing, or ahead of a move-in. It's for property managers and rental owners who need the unit ready and would rather not chase anyone to get there.

A typical turnover clean covers

Scope is confirmed for every unit before anything is arranged.

  1. A.01
    Kitchen

    Counters, sink, cabinet fronts and interiors, appliance exteriors. Inside the oven and refrigerator when agreed.

  2. A.02
    Bathrooms

    Toilet, tub or shower, sink, mirror, and fixtures.

  3. A.03
    Surfaces

    Dusting sills, baseboards, ledges, doors, and switch plates.

  4. A.04
    Floors

    Vacuuming and mopping throughout.

  5. A.05
    Left-behind trash

    Small items bagged and removed. Furniture or bulk items need a conversation first.

Need more than an ordinary clean — repairs, painting, hazardous cleanup? Mention it in your request. We'll tell you plainly whether it fits the pilot.

02 — How it works

Three steps. None of them are chasing.

Behind the scenes, software — including AI — helps us sort requests and line up schedules. People confirm every detail with you.

  1. 1

    Tell us what needs doing.

    Send the unit size, city, condition, and when you'd like it done. A short form — no phone tag.

  2. 2

    Agree on scope, price, and availability.

    We come back with what we can arrange: what's included, the price, and an appointment. Nothing is scheduled until you confirm.

  3. 3

    Get it done, with proof.

    The pro completes the work. For pilot jobs, we plan to share completion photos and follow up with you afterward.

06 — Questions

Fair questions.

Plain answers about an early pilot. If yours isn't here, put it in the notes of your request.

Is BookWorkGo a cleaning company?

No. We coordinate the job: we take your request, agree on the details with you, and arrange an independent local pro to do the work. Software, including AI, helps us sort requests and schedules; people confirm every detail.

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on unit size, condition, and scope. During the pilot there's no published price list — we agree on a price with you before any work is arranged.

When can the cleaning happen?

Your preferred date is a starting point, not an appointment. We check availability for each request, and nothing is scheduled until you confirm a specific date and time.

How will I know the work was done?

For pilot jobs, we plan to share completion photos after the visit and follow up with you to make sure the unit is where you need it.

How do keys and access work?

Access is arranged directly with you once a job is confirmed. Please don't put door codes or lockbox codes in the request form.

I'm a cleaning pro. What happens if I apply?

We review your experience, insurance, coverage area, rates, and capacity before arranging any work. Applying doesn't guarantee approval, jobs, or income, and you're never obligated to accept a job.
