What BookWorkGo does

BookWorkGo coordinates clearly scoped service jobs between business customers, such as property managers and rental owners, and independent local service providers ("service pros"). Our first service is ordinary cleaning of vacant rental units.

BookWorkGo is a coordinator. Unless a job confirmation says otherwise, the work itself is performed by an independent service pro, not by BookWorkGo employees. We use software, including artificial intelligence tools, to help organize requests and schedules. People at BookWorkGo confirm job details with you.

Early pilot

BookWorkGo is in an early pilot in Centennial, Greenwood Village, Lone Tree, and Parker, Colorado. Availability, scope, price, and appointment times are confirmed individually for each request. We may decline any request, and we don't guarantee availability, response times, or that a service pro can be arranged.

Parts of this website are in preview. Where a form says it doesn't send anything, nothing you enter there is transmitted to us.

Requests and appointments

A request, or a preferred date you give us, is not a booking. A job is scheduled only when we send you a confirmation that states the scope of work, the price, and the appointment date and time, and you accept it. The confirmation, together with these Terms, describes that job.

Please give us accurate information about the property, its condition, and access. If the property differs materially from what was described, the service pro or BookWorkGo may adjust the scope or price with your agreement, or decline to continue.

Prices and payment

We don't publish a price list during the pilot. The price for each job is agreed with you before work is scheduled. How and when payment is due will be stated in your job confirmation. This website does not collect payment information.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel a confirmed job, tell us as early as you can. Any cancellation or rescheduling terms that apply to a job, including any fees, will be stated in that job's confirmation before you accept it.

We may need to reschedule or cancel a job, for example if a service pro becomes unavailable or conditions at the property are unsafe. If that happens, we'll tell you promptly and work with you on a new time.

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide access to the property at the confirmed time, through a method we agree on directly. Don't send door codes or lockbox codes through website forms.
  • Make sure the unit is vacant, has working water and power unless otherwise agreed, and is safe to work in.
  • Tell us in advance about hazards, including mold, biohazards, pests, sharps, or structural problems. These are outside the scope of an ordinary clean unless confirmed in writing.
  • Remove or secure valuables and items you don't want handled or discarded.
  • Tell us promptly about any concern with completed work, so we can review it with the service pro.
